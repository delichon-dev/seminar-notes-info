# Seminar Notes

An iPhone note-taking app for talks, courses, and meetings, with the camera built into the note editor.

In development. Not yet on the App Store.

## What it does

- **Type notes and shoot slides on one screen.** The camera lives in the note; every shot lands at the cursor and you keep typing.
- **Burst capture.** One tap takes a short burst, so a slide hidden by the presenter in one frame is usually visible in another.
- **Straightened slides, on-device.** The best frame of each burst is perspective-corrected on your phone, with no cloud processing.
- **Your notes are yours.** Every talk is a plain folder: Markdown notes plus the photos. Export it as a zip to any app or cloud drive.

## Privacy

- No accounts
- No tracking
- No analytics
- No data collection by the app

All app data is stored locally on your device.
