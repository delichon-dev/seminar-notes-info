# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-25

## Summary
Seminar Notes is built to work without collecting personal data. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information. Your notes and photos stay on your device until you choose to export them.

## Data the app collects

- **None by default.**
- **Notes and photos** — only what you yourself type and photograph in the app. They are stored on your device in a plain folder per talk.

## Camera

The app uses the camera to photograph slides while you take notes. Camera access is requested the first time you open the capture screen. Photos are saved inside the app's own storage on your device. The app does not read or write your Photos library unless you explicitly import a photo from it.

## Analytics / tracking

- **None.**
- No third-party analytics SDKs.
- No advertising identifiers.

## Data storage

All app data — your notes, photos, and settings — is stored **locally on your device**. Each talk is a folder containing Markdown text and image files, which you can also see in the Files app under Seminar Notes.

iOS may include this app's local data in your device backup (iCloud or Finder). Backup behaviour is controlled by iOS, not by Seminar Notes.

## Network

- The app does not send your notes, photos, or any other personal data to any server.
- No background network features upload your data.

## Export and sharing

When you export a talk, the app creates a zip archive of that talk's folder and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (a cloud drive, an email, another app) is your choice, and that destination's privacy policy applies to the copy you send.

## Support

If you contact support (for example via email), you will share whatever information you include in your message. This information is used only to respond to your request.
