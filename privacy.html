Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026. Replaces the policy of 25 September 2026.

Summary

Seminar Notes is built to work without collecting personal data. The app has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, and no advertising. It does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information. Your notes, photos, slide text, transcripts, people, and receipts stay on your phone until you choose to export, share, or back them up.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you make in the app is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own folder, which you can also see in the Files app under Seminar Notes:

  • Notes and photos you type and take in the app, with the straightened copies of the slides and the text read from them.
  • Transcripts of talks you chose to transcribe, as text only. See Microphone below.
  • Seminars, organizers, locations, and people you add, and photos of business cards or badges you scanned.
  • Attachments and receipts you added to a talk or a seminar.
  • Settings, such as your languages and export choices.

iOS may include the app's data in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer). That backup is made and controlled by iOS and Apple, not by Seminar Notes.

What the app sends

Nothing, unless you ask it to. There is no background upload, no sync service of ours, and no telemetry. The app uses the network only in these cases, each one started by you:

  • Reading an event's web page. When you paste the address of an event's page and tap Read Web Page, the app loads that page from its server, once, to read the programme. The page's owner sees an ordinary page request from your phone.
  • Speech models. The first time you transcribe in a language, iOS downloads Apple's speech model for it from Apple. Transcription itself runs on the phone.
  • Looking up a location. When you type a place name on a location's page, the search is done by Apple Maps, as in the Maps app. When you tap Use My Position, the app reads your position once to save it with the location. It does not track your position.
  • Lifetime Access. The purchase is made through Apple's App Store. We receive no payment details and no name; Apple handles the transaction under its own privacy policy.
  • Sharing and exporting. When you share or export a talk, a PDF, a backup, or a calendar file, the app hands the file to iOS. Where it goes from there, such as a mail, a message, or a cloud drive, is your choice, and that destination's privacy policy applies to the copy you send.

Camera

The app uses the camera to photograph slides, business cards, badges, and receipts while you take notes. iOS asks for permission the first time you open the capture screen. Photos are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. The app reads your Photos library only when you choose a photo to import, through the system photo picker.

Microphone and speech recognition

Transcription is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on and you start it for a talk, the app listens through the microphone and turns the speech into text on the phone with Apple's on-device speech recognition. No sound is stored: the app keeps at most the last 90 seconds of audio in memory while it listens, and writes only the recognized text. No audio is written to a talk, to a backup, or to a file. Whether you may record a speaker is for you to judge; ask when in doubt.

On-device intelligence

Straightening, text recognition, and transcription use Apple's frameworks on the phone. On a phone with Apple Intelligence, the app may use Apple's on-device model to summarize a talk or to read an agenda. The model runs on the phone, and the app never sends your text to a cloud model. Text the model wrote is marked as generated, and the text it was made from stays in the app.

Calendar

When you add upcoming talks to your calendar, the app asks for write-only access and adds the events you chose. It cannot read your calendar.

People

Details of speakers and of the people you met come only from what you type, from what the speaker or the conference published, or from a business card or a badge you scanned. The app does not look people up on the web.

Analytics and tracking

  • None. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party code: the app uses only Apple's frameworks.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Support

If you write to us, we see what you put in your message, and we use it only to answer you. Contact: dev@delichon.no.
